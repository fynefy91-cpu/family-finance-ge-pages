Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. This policy describes the Family Finance GE personal-use utility and its Google Drive integration.

Who operates it

Family Finance GE is a personal household-finance workflow operated by the Google account holder, rather than a public financial institution or commercial data broker.

Information accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the utility uses Google Drive API read access to inspect designated private folders and read JSON files containing bank transaction notification messages. These files may contain transaction amounts, dates, merchant names, account-related references, and other text included in a notification.

Why the information is used

The messages are parsed to identify eligible household transactions, detect duplicates, and add financial records to the account holder's configured Actual Budget instance. Unsupported messages are not automatically posted as financial transactions. Authentication or verification messages are intended to be excluded from processing.

Storage and retention

Original cloud messages remain in the account holder's Google Drive. The cloud importer reads their contents into process memory and does not intentionally create separate local copies of the source JSON or raw SMS. Limited technical state or metadata may be retained locally for operation and troubleshooting. Actual Budget maintains the imported transactions and may maintain a local budget cache on the Mac. Retention of original files in Google Drive and imported records in Actual Budget is controlled by the account holder through those services.

Sharing and third parties

The utility does not sell personal data, share it for advertising, or use it for analytics or profiling. Data is transmitted as necessary between the account holder's Google Drive and their selected Actual Budget server. Those providers and the hosting of this public information page operate under their own privacy terms. No bank messages or financial data are included in this public website.

Google API access

Google Drive permission is requested for this personal workflow, preferably with the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. Access can be revoked by the Google account holder in Google Account security settings. The utility is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Security and control

OAuth credentials and account access tokens are not published in the website repository. The operator is responsible for protecting the Mac, Google account, connected services, and any local configuration files. The public website does not provide sign-in or accept financial submissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the functionality or data handling of the personal integration changes. The effective date will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For questions about this notice, use the project's GitHub Issues page. Do not disclose personal or financial details in public issues.
